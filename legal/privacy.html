Hosting

GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, which covers the transfer to the USA.

Counting visits

To learn what works, the page counts anonymous events such as "page viewed", "checkout started" and "payment completed". Each event adds 1 to a counter at Abacus (abacus.jasoncameron.dev), a free counting service that may be operated outside the EU. The request carries only the event name. Like any web request it reaches the service from your IP address; we never receive your IP address and build no profile. Nothing is stored on your device for counting, and no cookies are used. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in knowing how the site is used.

What is stored on your device

Only what the service you asked for needs (Section 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG), in your browser's local storage:
overduekit_form: the invoice details you typed, so the form is filled in next time.
overduekit_unlock: your unlock and PayPal order ID, so the paid version stays unlocked.
noble_exclude, only in the owner's own browser, so the owner's visits are not counted.

Payments

When you open the payment step, the PayPal script loads from paypal.com. PayPal processes your payment as an independent controller under its own privacy statement. We receive your order ID, the amount, and the payer details PayPal shares with sellers (name, email address, country). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of the contract) and Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR (tax law). We keep payment records for 10 years, as German tax law requires (Section 147 AO).

Email and support

If you write to us, we use your message and order ID only to answer you. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for questions about a purchase, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. We delete support messages after 2 years unless tax law requires longer storage.

Your content

What you type into the tool (client names, amounts, dates, payment details) is processed only in your browser and is never sent to us. It stays in your browser's local storage (see section 4) until you clear the site data.

Your rights

You have the right to access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20). Write to support.toolkits.desk@gmail.com. You may complain to a data protection authority, for example Die Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz Niedersachsen, Prinzenstraße 5, 30159 Hannover.

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): where we process data on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, you may object at any time for reasons arising from your particular situation. Write to support.toolkits.desk@gmail.com.

There is no automated decision-making or profiling. You do not have to give us any data to use the free parts of the site; buying requires the payment data PayPal shares.
