What you buy

Overdue Kit is a digital tool that runs in your web browser. The free part calculates statutory late-payment interest and writes the first reminder letter. The paid version unlocks letters 2 to 5, a calendar file with every send date and a print layout, for the invoices you enter. It is meant for businesses and freelancers chasing payment of business invoices.
