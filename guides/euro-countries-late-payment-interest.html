Late payment interest in euro countries that use the ECB rate plus 8

In Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, Slovenia and Spain, late business invoices carry statutory interest of the ECB reference rate plus 8 points. For a debt that became late between 1 July 2026 and 31 December 2026, that is 10,4% per year, plus at least EUR 40 compensation.

8 countries in Overdue Kit's checked data apply the EU minimum as it is: the ECB reference rate plus 8 percentage points. The ECB rate is the main refinancing rate in force on 1 January or 1 July of the half-year in which the debt became late.

What 10,4% means on a real invoice

On EUR 2.400,00 the interest is EUR 0,68 per day, or EUR 20,52 after 30 days. With the EUR 40 compensation the claim is EUR 2.460,52 after 30 days.

Questions

Which countries use the ECB rate plus 8 points?

In Overdue Kit's checked data: Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, Slovenia, Spain. Each one links to its national law below.

What is the rate right now?

For a debt that became late between 1 July 2026 and 31 December 2026: ECB reference rate 2,4% plus 8 = 10,4% per year.

Is the EUR 40 compensation the same in these countries?

The directive sets at least EUR 40 per late business invoice. The calculator adds EUR 40 for every EU country.
