The EUR 40 late payment compensation (and the UK's GBP 40 to 100)

When a business client pays an invoice late, EU law gives you at least EUR 40 per invoice for recovery costs, on top of interest. In the UK the fixed sum is GBP 40, 70 or 100, depending on the invoice amount.

EU: at least EUR 40

Article 6 of Directive 2011/7/EU gives a business creditor a fixed sum of at least EUR 40 for each late invoice, without sending a reminder first. It sits on top of the statutory interest.

UK: GBP 40, 70 or 100

Invoice amountFixed sum
Up to GBP 999,99GBP 40
GBP 1.000 to 9.999,99GBP 70
GBP 10.000 or moreGBP 100

Source: gov.uk, late commercial payments.

Example

Three invoices of EUR 800,00 each, all late, in a country on the EU minimum: 3 x EUR 40 = EUR 120 compensation, plus interest on each invoice.

Questions

How much can I add to a late invoice in the EU?

At least EUR 40 per late business invoice, as compensation for recovery costs (Directive 2011/7/EU, Article 6).

How much in the UK?

GBP 40 up to GBP 999,99, GBP 70 up to GBP 9.999,99, GBP 100 from GBP 10.000.

Is it per invoice or per client?

Per late invoice. Three late invoices from one client give three fixed sums.
